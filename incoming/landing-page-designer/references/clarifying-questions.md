# Clarifying Questions — a menu, not a script

Rules of use:
- **Ask only what the ingest left open.** Re-asking what the materials answered wastes the user's trust.
- **One short batch, aim ≤5 questions.** This is a direction check, not an interview. If almost everything is unclear, the real gap is app context — ask for a README/description first, not twenty questions.
- Anything still open after the batch → state the assumption explicitly in `strategy.md` and proceed.
- Tone selection (SKILL.md Phase 2) always happens, but costs no question slot when the recommendation is just confirmed.

## The question bank, by direction topic

**Tone / voice**
- Recommend a pack + one-line why; confirm or let them pick. (Always asked, usually as a confirmation.)
- Any banned words, reserved terms, or an existing brand-voice guide the copy must obey?

**Hook / differentiator**
- Of everything the app does, what's the one thing that makes someone say "finally"?
- Who is the page's primary visitor — and what have they already tried that failed them?
- What's the "after" state — 30 days in, what's different for the user?

**Proof points**
- What real proof exists today: rating, review count, downloads, press, testimonials, a founder story?
- If proof is thin: OK to ship with `[NEEDS PROOF]` placeholders, or should the page lean on honesty instead ("new app" framing)?

**Traffic source**
- Where will visitors mostly come from — paid ads, App Store search, social/organic, press, referral?
- If paid: what does the ad promise? (The hero must match it.)

**CTA / store specifics**
- iOS only, or iOS + Android? Released, or preorder/waitlist-to-launch on the store?
- Is the app free, freemium, paid, or subscription — and should the page say so? (Price surprise at the store kills the tap's value.)

**Constraints**
- Any sections the user definitely wants or definitely doesn't want?
- Any legal/compliance lines required (health claims, finance disclaimers, kids' privacy)?
